Gift-card review sentiment & emotion

One hundred and fifteen-thousand-odd Amazon Gift-Cards reviews, scored by an LLM and by an NRC emotion word list. The model never sees the star rating; the rating is only the ground truth it is scored against. Everything below is computed in your browser from the saved prediction file.

Built as part of MBAX 6418 · Assignment 1. Data: Amazon Reviews '23 — Gift Cards category (amazon-reviews-2023.github.io; McAuley Lab, UC San Diego).
